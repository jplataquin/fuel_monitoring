🎸 QUICK START GUIDE

  1. Toggle the POWER rocker switch to turn on the amplifier (glowing pilot lamp).
  2. Click the INPUT jack to play or pause the built-in guitar loop.
  3. Drag vertical on any gold-brushed knob to shape your distortion, sag, and resonance!
  4. Swap the routing between STUDIO mode (post-cabinet tail) and FX LOOP mode (vintage power-tube tails).
